About Dr. Sharma

Dr. Ajay Kumar Sharma is a dedicated scientist working at the intersection of chemistry, radiopharmaceutical development, and molecular imaging. His research focuses on designing novel PET imaging agents and radiotherapeutics for precision diagnosis and targeted treatment of cancers, including ovarian, pancreatic, neuroendocrine tumors, multiple myeloma, and bladder cancer.

His expertise spans solid-phase peptide synthesis, radiometal chelation, 18F-fluorosulfate chemistry, and translational PET imaging using small animal models. Dr. Sharma has contributed to pioneering peptide-based agents targeting unconventional receptors such as EphA2, PD-L1, CD38, and Nectin-4. His translational pipeline bridges the gap between chemical innovation and biological impact.

Dr. Sharma's research has been supported by the HERA Ovarian Cancer Foundation, ERF NET RF, and Colorectal Cancer Research Center of Excellence. He is actively involved in mentoring, grant writing, and manuscript preparation, committed to advancing cancer theranostics and building a collaborative, interdisciplinary lab environment.

Outside the lab, Ajay finds joy in mountain hiking, camping, and exploring scenic drives with his family. He believes in science with purpose and lives by the motto: "Chase curiosity, create impact."
