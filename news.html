Lab News and Updates

January 2025

🎉 Lab Website Launch

The Sharma Lab website officially goes live! Showcasing our research in radiopharmaceutical development, team achievements, and latest publications. Welcome to our digital home for cancer theranostics innovation.

December 2024

💰 Major Grant Success

HERA Ovarian Cancer Foundation Grant Awarded! Dr. Sharma receives significant funding ($150,000) to advance molecular radiotherapy research for ovarian cancer treatment. This grant will support the development of novel theranostic agents targeting ovarian cancer-specific biomarkers.

November 2024

📄 New Publication in Journal of Nuclear Medicine

Our latest work on CD38-targeted PET imaging agents for multiple myeloma has been published! This research demonstrates the potential for precision imaging in hematological malignancies.

October 2024

🏆 Multiple Grant Awards

Triple Success! Dr. Sharma's team receives:

  • NIH Trailblazer Award (R21) - "Development of an In Vivo Stable Fluorosulfate Group for Rapid and Late-Stage F-18 Labeling" (Under Review)
  • Colorectal Cancer Research Center of Excellence Pilot Award - Supporting innovative cancer imaging research
  • ERF NET RF Research Grant - Advancing nuclear medicine innovation
September 2024

📝 Manuscript Submission

"An EphA2-targeted theranostic for enhancing PDAC treatment" manuscript prepared and submitted to Molecular Pharmaceutics. This work represents a significant advancement in pancreatic cancer theranostics.

June 2024

🎤 SNMMI Annual Meeting Success

Young Investigator Award Winner! Dr. Sharma presents "Development of an in vivo stable fluorosulfate group for rapid F-18 labeling" at the Society of Nuclear Medicine and Molecular Imaging Annual Meeting in Toronto, Canada. Recognized with the prestigious Young Investigator Award.

🏢 Lab Establishment

Sharma Lab Officially Established! Dr. Ajay Kumar Sharma joins Johns Hopkins School of Medicine as Research Associate (Faculty) and establishes his independent research laboratory in Cancer Research Building II (CRB II), Room 490.
